About The Exultant

The Exultant is the official student publication of Holy Cross Academy, dedicated to providing quality journalism, creative expression, and a platform for the student voice. Founded on the principles of integrity, excellence, and service, our publication serves as the bridge between the student body, faculty, and administration.

Our team is composed of passionate student journalists, writers, editors, photographers, and artists who work tirelessly to bring the stories, events, and issues that matter to our school community. From breaking news and sports coverage to thought-provoking editorials and creative literary works, The Exultant covers it all.

Our Mission

To deliver accurate, relevant, and engaging journalism that informs and empowers the student body, while providing a creative platform that nurtures talent and promotes the values of truth, integrity, and community service.

Our Vision

To be the leading student publication that inspires excellence, fosters creativity, and serves as a lasting voice for the Holy Cross Academy community, both now and for generations to come.

Student Publication Unit

The Student Publication Unit is the official department of Holy Cross Academy responsible for overseeing and managing all student publications, including The Exultant. The unit ensures editorial standards, provides training and resources for student journalists, and promotes freedom of expression within the bounds of responsible journalism.

The unit is composed of the faculty adviser, editors-in-chief, section editors, staff writers, and contributing writers from various departments within the school.
